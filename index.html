1. Introduction

HeartNote is a private diary app. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. In short, your diary stays on your device and is encrypted there. Nothing is uploaded unless you are a Premium subscriber and you actively turn on Cloud Backup.

By using HeartNote, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. Information That Stays on Your Device

The following information is stored only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Diary entries: Title, text, entry date, mood, tags, favorite status, and creation and edit times.
  • Photo attachments: Images you add to an entry.
  • App lock data: Your PIN, stored in protected form, and your biometric-unlock preference.
  • Settings and consent choices: For example, whether you have agreed to Cloud Backup.

The text of each diary entry and your photo attachments are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, using a key held in the Android Keystore. The key is tied to your device, and we never receive it. We do not have access to the contents of your diary, and we cannot recover it if you lose your device or clear the app's data.

Biometrics: If you enable fingerprint or face unlock, authentication is handled entirely by Android. HeartNote only receives a success or failure result. We never receive, see, or store your fingerprint or face data.

Screenshots: The app blocks screenshots, screen recording, and the recent-apps preview of diary screens to protect your privacy.

Android backup: HeartNote opts out of Android's automatic cloud backup, so your diary is not copied into a Google or device backup without your knowledge.

3. Cloud Backup (Premium, Optional, Off by Default)

Cloud Backup is available only to Premium subscribers and is never enabled automatically. Before anything is uploaded, you must give explicit consent in the app.

If you turn it on, the app does the following:

  • Creates an anonymous account: Firebase Authentication creates an account without requiring a name, email address, or password. It provides your backup with a random unique ID.
  • Uploads your diary entries: Entries are uploaded to Firebase Realtime Database under your unique ID. Access rules are configured so each account can access only its own data.

What is uploaded for each entry:

  • The entry text in encrypted form, along with the value needed to decrypt it. The decryption key stays on your device.
  • The entry's title, mood, tags, date, favorite status, timestamps, a reference to any photo attachment, and an identifier for the device that last saved it.
Entry metadata such as titles, moods, tags, dates, and timestamps is not end-to-end encrypted. It is protected in transit and by Google's server-side security, but it is readable by the backend service. Avoid putting highly sensitive information in titles or tags when using Cloud Backup. Photo attachments are not uploaded in the current version and remain on your device.

Turning Off Cloud Backup and Deleting Data

You can turn Cloud Backup off at any time. This stops future uploads. To remove data that has already been uploaded, use Settings → Privacy → Delete Cloud Data. This deletes your cloud diary backup. Residual copies may remain in Google's systems for a limited time before they are overwritten.

4. Purchases and Premium Status

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment card or other payment details.

To determine whether you are Premium, the app:

  • Asks Google Play Billing for your current purchases on your device.
  • Sends the purchase token and product ID to our purchase-verification function using Firebase Cloud Functions, which checks with Google Play that the purchase is valid.

We use this information only to confirm your Premium entitlement and keep ads away from Premium users.

5. Advertising

Free users see ads served by Google AdMob, including banner ads and a full-screen ad after saving an entry. Premium users do not see ads.

To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and operating-system details, and ad interaction data. Ads may be personalized. Google handles this data for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention under its Privacy Policy.

Your diary content is never shared with advertisers or the ad SDK.

You can limit ad personalization or reset your advertising ID in your Android settings, such as Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device. You can also remove ads by upgrading to Premium.

6. Crash Reports

HeartNote uses Firebase Crashlytics to identify and fix crashes. When the app crashes, it may send technical information such as the device model, Android version, app version, a stack trace, and a randomly generated installation identifier.

The app is designed not to place diary content, PINs, or encryption keys in crash logs.

7. Exporting Your Diary

The Export diary feature creates a ZIP file containing your entries in readable form, including decrypted text. The file is saved wherever you choose and is not encrypted. HeartNote does not send it anywhere.

Please store the exported file somewhere safe because anyone with access to it may be able to read its contents.

8. Permissions the App Uses

  • Internet and network state: For Premium Cloud Backup, purchase verification, ads, and crash reporting. The app works fully offline without them.
  • Biometric: To let you unlock the app with your fingerprint or face.
  • Notifications: To show notifications if you allow them.

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera, or SMS.

9. Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that operate parts of the app, as described below:

  • Google Firebase: Authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Functions, and Crashlytics for Cloud Backup, purchase verification, and crash reports.
  • Google Play: Purchases and subscriptions.
  • Google AdMob: Advertising for free users.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or security of users or the public.

10. Data Retention and Your Choices

  • On-device data: Stays until you delete entries, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.
  • Cloud backup data: Stays until you delete it using Delete Cloud Data or it is otherwise removed under our backup-retention practices.
  • Crash and ad data: Is retained by Google according to its own retention policies.
  • Consent: You can withdraw Cloud Backup consent at any time in Settings.

Because we do not collect your name or email for anonymous cloud accounts, we usually cannot link cloud data to you personally. Please use the in-app deletion tools. If you have trouble, contact us using the email address provided above.
