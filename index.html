1. Introduction

HeartNote is a private diary app. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. In short, your diary stays on your device and is encrypted there. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to back up to your own Google Drive.

By using HeartNote, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. Information That Stays on Your Device

The following information is stored only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Diary entries: Title, text, entry date, mood, tags, favorite status, and creation and edit times.
  • Photo attachments: Images you add to an entry (one per entry on the free plan, up to 10 with Premium).
  • App lock data: Your PIN, stored only as a salted, hashed value, and your biometric-unlock preference.
  • Settings: For example, your reminder time, your chosen theme, and your backup preferences.

The text of each diary entry and your photo attachments are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, using a key held in the Android Keystore. The key is tied to your device, and we never receive it. We do not have access to the contents of your diary, and we cannot recover it if you lose your device or clear the app's data.

Biometrics: If you enable fingerprint or face unlock, authentication is handled entirely by Android. HeartNote only receives a success or failure result. We never receive, see, or store your fingerprint or face data.

Screenshots: The app blocks screenshots, screen recording, and the recent-apps preview of diary screens to protect your privacy.

Android backup: HeartNote opts out of Android's automatic cloud backup, so your diary is not copied into a Google or device backup without your knowledge.

Photos: You choose each photo with Android's photo picker. HeartNote does not scan or access the rest of your gallery.

3. Google Drive Backup (Optional, Off by Default)

Backing up to Google Drive is optional and never happens unless you start it. Manual backup, restore and delete are free. Automatic daily backup is a Premium feature.

If you use it, the app does the following:

  • Asks for Google's permission: You choose a Google account and approve the Google Drive "app data" permission (drive.appdata). This permission lets HeartNote use only its own hidden folder in your Drive. HeartNote cannot see, open, or change any of your other Drive files, and the folder does not appear in your normal Drive.
  • Uploads your diary to your own Drive: Your backup file is stored in that hidden folder, under your own Google account. It goes directly from your phone to Google. It does not pass through any server operated by us, and we do not receive a copy.

What is uploaded:

  • Your diary entries: title, text, mood, tags, dates, favorite status, and timestamps.
  • The photo attachments of those entries.
The backup file is not separately encrypted by HeartNote. It is protected in transit and by Google's security for your account, but anyone who can sign in to your Google account may be able to read it. Keep your Google account secure, for example with 2-step verification.

Restoring, Turning Off, and Deleting Your Backup

You can restore your diary from your backup on a new phone by signing in to the same Google account. You can turn automatic backup off at any time, which stops future uploads. To remove your backup, use Settings → Google Drive backup → Delete backup. You can also revoke HeartNote's access in your Google Account permissions. Deleting data on your device does not delete your Drive backup, and deleting the backup does not delete the diary on your device.

HeartNote's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The information is used only to provide the backup and restore feature you asked for. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with anyone else.

4. Purchases and Premium Status

Subscriptions and purchases (Monthly, Yearly, and Forever) are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment card or other payment details.

To determine whether you are Premium, the app asks Google Play Billing on your device for your current purchases. We use this information only to unlock Premium features and keep ads away from Premium users. We do not run a server that stores your purchases.

5. Advertising

Free users see ads served by Google AdMob. Premium users do not see ads.

To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and operating-system details, and ad interaction data. Ads may be personalized. Google handles this data for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention under its Privacy Policy.

Your diary content is never shared with advertisers or the ad SDK.

You can limit ad personalization or reset your advertising ID in your Android settings, such as Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device. You can also remove ads by upgrading to Premium.

6. Daily Reminder

If you allow notifications and turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a notification on your device at the time you choose. It is created entirely on your device, uses generic text, and never contains your diary content. You can change the time or turn it off in Settings.

7. Exporting Your Diary

The Export diary feature creates a ZIP file containing your entries in readable form, including decrypted text. The file is saved wherever you choose and is not encrypted. HeartNote does not send it anywhere.

Please store the exported file somewhere safe because anyone with access to it may be able to read its contents.

8. Permissions the App Uses

  • Internet and network state: For Google Drive backup, Google Play purchases, and ads. The diary works fully offline without them.
  • Biometric: To let you unlock the app with your fingerprint or face.
  • Notifications: To show your daily reminder if you allow it.
  • Run after restart: To set your daily reminder again after your phone restarts.

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera, or SMS.

9. Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your personal information. HeartNote does not run its own servers and does not receive your diary. Data goes only to the following Google services that operate parts of the app:

  • Google Drive: Your optional backup, stored in your own Google account.
  • Google Play: Purchases and subscriptions.
  • Google AdMob: Advertising for free users.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or security of users or the public.

10. Data Retention and Your Choices

  • On-device data: Stays until you delete entries, use Delete all local data in Settings, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.
  • Google Drive backup: Stays in your Google account until you delete it using Delete backup in the app or in your Google Account.
  • Ad data: Is retained by Google according to its own retention policies.

Because we do not collect your name or email, and do not receive your diary, we usually cannot access or delete your data for you. Please use the in-app tools. If you have trouble, contact us using the email address provided above.

11. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data; object to or restrict certain processing; and withdraw consent.

Most of these actions can be performed directly in the app by editing or deleting entries, exporting your diary, deleting your Drive backup, or turning off backup. For other requests, email us and we will respond within a reasonable time and within any period required by applicable law. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

12. Security

We use safeguards that include encryption of diary text and images at rest, a key store provided by Android, an app lock with a PIN and optional biometrics, and encrypted network connections when talking to Google services.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Children's Privacy

HeartNote is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum age of digital consent in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

14. International Transfers

Google's services may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, Google relies on recognized transfer mechanisms to protect your data.
